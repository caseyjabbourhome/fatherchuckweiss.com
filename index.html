About the Book

Jesus wanted, most of all, for people to follow him. Some of the loudest professed Christians claim to follow Jesus while doing the exact opposite. As the Church finds its way back to following Jesus, the world will become a much more loving place.

The book is made up of two parts:

Part One

What Jesus Really Wanted

Makes the case that what Jesus wanted most was for people to follow him; unpacks what Jesus meant by “loving God with heart, soul, mind, and strength”; explores the connection between The Spirit of the Law and Consequentialist Ethics; and argues that following Jesus is both personal and political.

Part Two

Following Jesus with My Vote

Delves into what following Jesus might mean in terms of eight controversial political matters.

After each chapter, there are Reflection Questions for individuals or groups to consider their own beliefs and behavior. The book is both honest about the challenges of a Church gone astray, and hopeful about a future in which Christians follow Jesus better.

About the Author

Fr. Chuck Weiss has been privileged to serve The Episcopal Church in parish, camp, and school settings for more than thirty years. He has been honored to explore our sacred story, and the deepest spiritual questions of our lives, with countless people. He has also laughed a lot along the way! Fr. Chuck is convinced that Jesus, most of all, wanted people to follow him. Bringing his background in psychology and theology, along with a multitude of personal stories, and a sense of humor, he hopes in his book to help people do just that. He is absolutely convinced that our individual lives, and the world around us, will be better if we do.

Fr. Chuck is happily retired in his hometown of Pittsburgh, PA, with his beloved wife and cats, just down the road from the world’s most beautiful granddaughter and her parents. He and his wife regret that their beautiful daughter is far too happy with her husband in Seattle. Fr. Chuck enjoys playing and watching way too much tennis, reading science fiction and mysteries, laughing with friends, occasionally filling in at local churches, volunteering, and discussing with his wife what they want to be when they grow up.

If, after reading the book, you would like to continue the conversation, Fr. Chuck is available to preach, teach, or lead retreats. Feel free to reach out for more information.
