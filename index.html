About the Book

Jesus wanted, most of all, for people to follow him. Some of the loudest professed Christians claim to follow Jesus while doing the exact opposite. As the Church finds its way back to following Jesus better, the world will become a much more loving place.

I explain how in two parts. Part One, What Jesus Really Wanted, makes the case that what Jesus wanted most was for people to follow him; unpacks what Jesus meant by “loving God with heart, soul, mind, and strength.”

About the Author

Fr. Chuck Weiss has been privileged to serve The Episcopal Church in parish, camp, and school settings for more than thirty years. He has been honored to explore our sacred story, and the deepest spiritual questions of our lives, with countless people. He has also laughed a lot along the way! Fr. Chuck is convinced that Jesus, most of all, wanted people to follow him.

Bringing his background in psychology and theology, along with a multitude of personal stories, he hopes in his book to help people do just that. He is absolutely convinced that our individual lives, and the world around us, will be better if we do.

Fr. Chuck is happily retired in his hometown of Pittsburgh, PA, with his beloved wife and cats, just down the road from the world’s most beautiful granddaughter and her parents. He and his wife regret that their beautiful daughter is far too happy with her husband in Seattle.
